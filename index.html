cafe lp

Two cafe landing pages made from a single prompt, filed here exactly as each model generated them. Each is one self-contained HTML file.

  1. KOMOREBI CAFE GLM-5.3-Flash-FP4-Local Concept, menu and store information for a cafe lit through leaves, with a reservation call to action.
  2. KOKAGE COFFEE DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 Concept, menu, gallery, access and reservation sections for a cafe in the shade of a tree.

Source is kept at github.com/making/cafe-lp.
